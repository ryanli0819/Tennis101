## Format (`window.SERVE_MOTIONS[player][serve]`)
- `frames[]`: `{ i, t, j }`, where `j` maps a joint to `[x, y]`.
- Units are **body heights**. Multiply by the player's `heightM` to get metres.
  - `x`: sideways from the hips, positive = the player's **right**. Mirrored from the front-view video, because the game sees Sinner from behind.
  - `y`: height above the ground.
- Joints: `nose, neck, left/right Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, root, left/right Hip, Knee, Ankle`. Missing joints hold their last known position.
- `events` (seconds): `start` 1.31 (toss begins), `tossRelease` 1.60, `trophy` 1.98, `contact` 2.25, `finish` 2.71.

## How the game uses it now
- `index.html` → `motionPose()` blends frames, `poseToWorld()` scales to metres, `stepMotion()` plays the serve (ball in the left hand → toss → contact), and `drawCapturedFigure()` draws a first, simple pixel figure.
- Contact lands at about 2.95 m for Sinner (1.91 m tall), which is realistic.
- **Player Artist:** `drawCapturedFigure()` is the placeholder you replace. Move it into `src/players/` and draw the detailed sprite and racket on these exact joints.

## Known limits
- Around contact (2.1–2.4 s) the arms are partly rebuilt: whichever arm is on top is shown as a straight line to the hand.
- Hip height during the jump is approximate, because Sinner also moves toward the camera.
- Only the front view is used. The overhead clip is still to be cleaned (Serve Analyst).
