## Format (`window.SERVE_MOTIONS[player][serve]`)
- `frames[]`: `{ t, joints }` for the captured set, or `{ i, t, j }` for the older flat clips. `motionPose()` reads either.
- `joints` maps a name to `{ x, y, z }` in **body heights**. Multiply by `heightM` for metres.
  - `x`: sideways from the hips, positive = the player's **right** (the source package stored +x as the server's left; that axis is flipped on the way in).
  - `y`: height above the ground.
  - `z`: into the court. Monocular depth is the weak axis. The pixel figure still uses x and y, and adds the usual side-on depth.
- Older `j` maps a joint to `[x, y]` only (no z). Same x and y meaning.
- Joints: `nose, head, neck, chest, pelvis, root, left/right Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, Hip, Knee, Ankle, Toe`, plus ears, heels and index fingers on the captured set. Missing joints hold their last known position.
- `events` (seconds from the start of that clip): `start`, `tossRelease`, `trophy`, `contact`, `finish`.
- `speed_kmh`, `spin_rpm` and `serveType` are `null`. Do not fill them in. `kneeBend` and `backArch` are monocular estimates clamped to 0..1; the unclamped number is `kneeBendRaw` / `backArchRaw`.
- The toss in the source footage is the tossing wrist, not a tracked ball. The game keeps its own toss.
- `hand` is `"R"` or `"L"`. Shelton, Nadal and Tien are already left-handed in the joints, so the game does not mirror them again.
- `reused: true` means that player had only one checked serve (Fils) and the second serve plays the same frames.

## How the game uses it now
- `index.html` → `motionPose()` blends frames, `poseToWorld()` scales to metres, `stepMotion()` plays the serve (ball in the tossing hand → toss → contact), and `drawCapturedFigure()` draws the figure. A left-hander tosses with the right hand and hits with the left. While that serve is playing, `getPose(i)` returns the captured joints in court metres.
- Contact height follows the measured hitting wrist plus the game's racket length. The footage's speed, spin and serve type are not used.
- **Player Artist:** `drawCapturedFigure()` is the placeholder you replace. Move it into `src/players/` and draw the detailed sprite and racket on these exact joints.

## Known limits
- The live set is monocular 3D. Depth is the weak axis, Fils is a single front-view serve, Nakashima is 2021–22 practice (one low-confidence clip kept out of the match), and Kyrgios's underarm was not captured. Toss-to-contact time is the package's assumed 1.00 s, not a radar time.
- Around contact (2.1–2.4 s) on the older flat clips, the arms are partly rebuilt: whichever arm is on top is shown as a straight line to the hand.
- Hip height during the jump is approximate, because Sinner also moves toward the camera.
- Only the front view is used. The overhead clip is still to be cleaned (Serve Analyst).
