## Format (`window.STROKE_MOTIONS[player][family][k]`) — same shape as SERVE_MOTIONS
- `STROKE_MOTIONS[playerId] = { forehand: [Motion], backhand: [Motion], slice: [Motion], gaps: [...], rejected: [...] }`.
- Motion: `{ frames:[{ i, t, j, c }], events:{ start, unitTurn, backswing, forward, contact, finish }, hand, grip, view, source, ... }`.
  - `frames[].j` maps a joint to `[x, y]` exactly like SERVE_MOTIONS. `i` = frame in the local clip; `t` = seconds from `events.start` (video time).
  - Joints: SERVE_MOTIONS set (`nose, neck, left/right Shoulder, Elbow, Wrist, root, left/right Hip, Knee, Ankle`) **plus** `left/right Hand` (index knuckle), `left/right Heel`, `left/right Toe` for the full chain (hands, feet). `neck` = shoulder midpoint (chest line). `root` = mid-hip, x = 0 by definition.
  - `c` (optional, new) = MediaPipe visibility per joint (0–1). Readers that ignore it see the SERVE shape.
- Units: **body heights** (1.0 = standing height). Multiply by `heightM` (Anatomy RD.2 settled, SOURCED: Sinner 1.91 m, Alcaraz 1.83 m) for metres. Because the normalization is per take in pixels, `heightM` only converts to metres; it does not change the body-height numbers.
  - `x`: sideways from the hips, **+ = the player's right, as seen from behind** (match camera). Behind clips are not mirrored; the one front clip (alcaraz_bh_5) is mirrored.
  - `y`: height above the ground. Ground = the lowest foot point in that frame (ESTIMATED: one foot on the ground; wrong while airborne, e.g. alcaraz_fh_5 landing).
  - **No depth (z).** Monocular depth is unreliable and is not exported (`depth: null`). Forward/back (toward the net) is NOT measured.
- `hand` "R" for both (SOURCED, ATP hero JSON `PlayHand: Right-Handed`). `grip`: backhand "2H" SOURCED (ATP hero JSON `BackHand: Two-Handed` + ATP 2026 media guide "PLAYS: Right-handed · Two-handed backhand"), and the wrist gap at contact (0.07–0.24 H) agrees. Forehand "1H" and slice "1H" are **MEASURED (visual)** — no URL states them (see open question in GAPS).
- `events` (seconds from start): contact is **VISUAL** (dense real stills, ±3 frames; slow slice ±10). start/unitTurn/backswing/forward/finish are **VISUAL** from 16-still timelines (±6 frames, ±12 for Alcaraz zIr116qDPus, ±25 for the ultra-slow slice); the rule values are kept in `qa.autoEvents`.
- `timeScale: null`: the clips are slow-motion compilations with an **unknown** slow-motion factor. `t` is video seconds. Re-timing to real speed is a GAME-DESIGN step for the integrator (e.g. scale forward→contact to a chosen real duration) — not done here, not invented.
- `speedKmh`, `spinRpm`: null (not measurable from pose; no URL).
- `source`: YouTube URL (link only), `timeRangeS` (source seconds), catalog range/view/stroke, oEmbed title, `quality` (medium/low), `qualityReasons`. Compilations: event and match-vs-practice unstated; not presented as match-level.
- `qa`: body px, omitted frames (`collapsedOmitted`, `spikeOmitted`), bone-length CV, wrist visibility, per-take summary metrics.

Chain notes (read with the compare sheets): the narrowest-shoulder frame comes before the narrowest-hip frame in 13 of 23 takes (same frame in 5, after in 5) — a weak, ESTIMATED sign of shoulders leading the coil (2-D width only, sign-less). The free arm travels with the racket in the FH unit turn (visual) and stays on the racket through the 2H backhand (wrist gap ≤ 0.24 H at contact). In the slice the left hand releases at contact and the arm extends back as a counterbalance: wrist gap 0.12–0.13 H at contact → 0.62–0.66 H at the finish in alcaraz_sl_1 and sl_3; in sl_2 the arms are hidden in front of the body after contact and the release is not measurable. Low ball → lower hips: the lowest backhand contact (sinner_bh_3, wrist 0.39 H) has the largest hip drop (0.11 H ≈ 21 cm at 1.91 m) and the deepest knee (79°). Across all 23 takes the correlation of contact height with lowest hip height is weak (r = −0.08), because camera height and footwork differ between clips — treat "squat follows ball height" as a within-take observation, not a fitted rule.

Hip heights are not comparable between clips filmed from different camera heights (Alcaraz's qkQmT4fXKD8 FH start at 0.38–0.42 H vs Sinner's u6lybamSAQ0 at 0.47–0.52 H is partly camera geometry). Turn angles in `measurements.json` (`shoulderTurnEst`, `hipTurnEst` = arccos of apparent width ratio) are ESTIMATED, sign-less and saturate near 90°. 2-D elbow angles are unreliable when the arm points along the camera axis (e.g. sinner_fh_1 frame 489 reads 4°).

## How the game should use it
- Draw from behind with these `[x,y]` exactly like SERVE_MOTIONS (`motionPose()` / `poseToWorld()` pattern). Each motion must be re-timed (GAME-DESIGN) before play.
- `lowConfidence: true` takes (front view; or hitting wrist hidden by the body through the swing — common for 2H backhands filmed from behind) should not be the default take.
- Left-handers (Shelton, Tien, Nadal): not in this increment. Rule for later: mirror x for a left-hander only when the source is a right-hander's clip being reused — never copy a right-hander's motion as a left-hander's own.

## Known limits
- Contact frames are visual at 60 fps output of slow-motion video, ±3 frames; ball–string contact is not always visible.
- Five Alcaraz BH windows begin with the take-back under way (start = first frame of the analysed window) except alcaraz_bh_5.
- Alcaraz zIr116qDPus "back view" is an oblique behind-right camera: turned-stance frames look side-on (legs overlap). Visually checked from behind: not a collapse, but low confidence.
- Bone lengths vary 16–46% (CV) across keyframes because 2-D projection foreshortens limbs during rotation; this is projection, not twitch. The rig should keep its own bone lengths (Anatomy RD.2) and use these as directions.
