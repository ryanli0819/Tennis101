# Phone moves without buttons
- Landed: not on main yet (branch agent/controls-designer-hq-20261118-002, HQ-20261118-002); Version Keeper: set the hour it merges
- Source: Controls Designer, PR pending review
- Change: On a phone, a double-tap does a split step, a short flick down slides in the direction you are moving, and a short flick sideways arms a slice for one shot (the reach ring around your player turns steady white while it is armed; flick sideways again to cancel). Dragging with one finger still moves your player, a second finger no longer steals the move stick, and the Space, Shift and E keys are unchanged. This lets the Slide, Split step and Slice buttons be removed during play.
